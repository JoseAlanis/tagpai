    Codebook

    How it works

    1. The conversation appears one reply at a time, the therapist on the left and the patient on the right. Read a reply, then press Space or the arrow at the bottom for the next. Earlier replies stay above.
    2. Select words and pick a colour, or press its number. Then say in a few words why you marked it. Some colours need a note; one of the suggested words counts.
    3. Click a highlight to see, change or remove what is on it. One passage can carry several colours.
    4. Under some replies is the raw output: what the model wrote before it was cleaned. The underlined part never reached the other speaker. Read it, and mark problems there with Form.
    5. After the last reply come your marks and a few questions. A reply you moved past without marking counts as "nothing here".

    Saving and batches

    The browser keeps a copy after every change. Save writes the file you send back; a dot on it means some changes are not in a file yet. New sessions come as a batch file, which you add on the home page. Please do not write anything about yourself or other people in notes.
